Most Important Visual Takeaway

The sector is healthy enough to attract strategic investment, but unhealthy enough operationally that execution quality now determines winners and losers. The October 24 scorecard: the science is strong (ESMO 2026 delivered 55% cORR for a PRAME cell therapy and 29.2% complete responses for first-line TIL), but the risk layer moved against the sector — a patient death in BMS's autoimmune CAR-T trial, a second death from tumor lysis in GPC3 CAR-T, and Roche abandoning the off-the-shelf CAR-T in a $1.5B deal. Meanwhile the market structure question sharpened: only about two in ten CAR-T-eligible patients receive treatment, with travel and reimbursement friction, not manufacturing capacity, the binding constraint.

🌐 Peer-reviewed clinical studyOctober 25, 2026
CAR-T failure mechanism: CD8 exhaustion from lost IL-2 signaling, reversed by pegenzileukin in the lab

Blood (MD Anderson + Sanofi, online October 23, 2026): scRNA-seq of tumors at progression from CART19 found clonally expanded, proliferating CART19 cells with transcriptional dysfunction features in a subset of tumors. Iterative rechallenge reproduced the signature with reduced IL-2 signaling; the non-alpha IL-2 receptor agonist pegenzileukin (THOR-707 / SAR444245) prevented dysfunction and improved expansion and tumor control. In a post-CART19 progression cell-line model: CART19 alone → transient response, then progression and secondary CNS relapse; CART19 + pegenzileukin → complete tumor eradication. Status: preclinical — in vitro and cell-line model, no patient data, no combination trial readout. Why it matters: expansion potential and cytokine responsiveness become release-testable product attributes; and the franchise now pairs a cell product with a non-cell biologic.

🌐 Peer-reviewed clinical studyOctober 25, 2026
CD19 CAR-T in lymphoma: equal response, unequal survival — organ-specific response patterns

Retrospective single-centre study, 109 consecutive r/r LBCL patients, CD19-directed CAR-T, January 2019 to May 2025. Depth of response comparable nodal vs extranodal (minus 62.1% vs 65.6% at first follow-up; minus 77.9% vs 78.1% at second), but median PFS 306.5 days nodal vs 90 days extranodal. Extranodal at baseline in 65/109 (59.6%) with higher starting burden (median SPD 3573 vs 1998 mm²). Baseline tumor burden independently associated with PFS and OS (HR 1.17 / 1.21 per doubling). Pleural, hepatobiliary, adrenal worst-associated; pleural independently associated with PFS and OS. Caveats: subgroups n = 7–19, exploratory, possible differential attrition. Why it matters: the limiter looks like starting tumor burden, not response — durability in extranodal/pleural disease is the open question.

BMC Cancer: pre-infusion score predicts post-CAR-T blood count crashes

96 patients, Beijing Tongren Hospital. Grade 3–4 neutropenia peaked at day 4 in 71.9%, with 34.7% still severe at day 28. CAR-HEMATOTOX score was the only significant predictor of severe early neutropenia (aOR 4.04) and the strongest predictor of early and late thrombocytopenia (aOR 8.22 / 4.20).
