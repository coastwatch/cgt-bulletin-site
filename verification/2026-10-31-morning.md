### MHRA extends Casgevy to eligible UK patients aged 5+

- **Primary source:** [MHRA announcement on GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/mhra-approves-gene-editing-treatment-for-children-aged-five-and-over-with-serious-inherited-blood-disorders), published October 30, 2026; the authorization extension was granted October 29 to Vertex Pharmaceuticals (Europe) Ltd.
- The extension allows eligible children aged five and older with severe sickle-cell disease or transfusion-dependent beta-thalassemia to receive exagamglogene autotemcel (Casgevy). The prior UK authorization covered patients aged 12 and older.
- The agency describes ex-vivo editing of the patient's bone-marrow stem cells, conditioning, and reinfusion. The announcement does not report NHS reimbursement, access, or uptake; the item makes no such claim.
- **Duplicate check:** the site already covers the separate FDA expansion to age 2. This item is specifically a new UK authorization change and is not a duplicate of the US regulatory action.
