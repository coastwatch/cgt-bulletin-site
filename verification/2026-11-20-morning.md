### 1. MiSLE missed the pooled primary endpoint in a Phase II trial of umbilical-cord MSCs for SLE

- **Primary source:** [Lupus Foundation of America / PR Newswire, “Lupus Foundation of America-Supported Phase II Trial Shows Promise for Mesenchymal Stromal Cells as a Potential New Lupus Therapy”](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lupus-foundation-of-america-supported-phase-ii-trial-shows-promise-for-mesenchymal-stromal-cells-as-a-potential-new-lupus-therapy-302945061.html) — HTTP 200, body read directly. Release dated November 19, 2026, 10:00 AM ET. The result had been presented at ACR Convergence on November 10.
- **Independent conference record:** [ACR 2026 abstract, “Mesenchymal Stromal Cells for Refractory Lupus: Results from the Phase II Multicenter, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial”](https://acrabstracts.org/abstract/mesenchymal-stromal-cells-for-refractory-lupus-results-from-the-phase-ii-multicenter-double-blind-placebo-controlled-trial/) — HTTP 200, abstract read directly. Confirms the primary and dose-specific analyses.
- The randomized, placebo-controlled study tested a single infusion of allogeneic umbilical-cord MSCs in adults with active SLE despite standard treatment. Of 87 randomized, 81 received infusion: 54 MSC (27 per dose) and 27 placebo. The primary analysis included 80 after one placebo withdrawal.
- The pooled Week-24 modified SRI-4 primary endpoint was not met: 21/54 (38.9%) in the pooled MSC group versus 6/26 (23.1%) on placebo; adjusted p=0.156. The high-dose secondary comparison was 13/27 (48.1%) versus 6/26 (23.1%), p=0.054, below conventional statistical significance. The item leads with the primary failure and identifies the higher-dose result as secondary and unconfirmed. The LFA release says a confirmatory higher-dose trial is in development; no follow-on study is claimed registered.
- Safety was followed through Week 52. The release reports serious adverse events in 27.8% of MSC recipients and 14.8% of placebo recipients, none attributed to study drug; one placebo participant died and no MSC recipients died. These details were not used to imply a treatment-related mortality signal.
- The LFA release and ACR abstract phrase the per-protocol high-dose comparator differently. The item deliberately omits those per-protocol figures rather than reconciling incompatible denominators from the two sources.
- **Registry (ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API; robots.txt read this session before the detailed record read):** [NCT02633163](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02633163) — HTTP 200. Completed Phase 2; lead sponsor Medical University of South Carolina; 81 actual participants; primary completion August 5, 2025; study completion February 3, 2026; latest record update posted August 24, 2026. The record describes one IV infusion of umbilical-cord MSCs for SLE. ACR abstract reports nine U.S. centers. Registry status dates and enrollment are record fields, not new results.
- **Timing and editorial scope:** The LFA release predates the previous bulletin cutoff (Nov. 19, 6:00 PM ET). It was absent from the prior evening page, so this is disclosed as a late-found catch-up—not presented as a new post-cutoff development. The ACR presentation itself was Nov. 10.
