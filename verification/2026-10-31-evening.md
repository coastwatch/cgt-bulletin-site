### 1. FDA grants accelerated approval to INOVIO's VOCERYQ (DNA immunotherapy, recurrent respiratory papillomatosis)

- **Primary source:** [INOVIO press release, PR Newswire, October 30, 2026, 6:01 PM ET](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/inovio-announces-fda-accelerated-approval-of-voceryq-an-immunotherapy-to-reduce-the-frequency-of-surgery-in-adults-with-recurrent-respiratory-papillomatosis-rrp-302925406.html) — HTTP 200, body read directly.
- VOCERYQ (doruxapapogene ralaplasmid and lalikinogene sifuplasmid-gxfd), previously INO-3107: plasmid DNA immunotherapy targeting HPV-6/HPV-11, delivered with the CELLECTRA electroporation device. Accelerated approval to reduce the frequency of surgery in adults with recurrent respiratory papillomatosis (RRP). Company statement: first FDA-approved DNA medicine.
- **Supporting data:** RRP-001, 32 adults with two or more surgeries in the prior year; 26 required three or more excisions in the 12 months before first dose (median 4.5, range 3–8) and formed the efficacy population; 7 of 26 (27%, 95% CI 12–48%) achieved complete response = no surgical excision over 12 months. Continued approval contingent on verification of clinical benefit; draft confirmatory protocol due end-2026, final protocol alignment mid-2027. All 32 completed; most treatment-related AEs grade 1–2 and transient; no grade 4/5 common AEs.
- **Registry:** NCT04398433 — RRP-001, completed open-label Phase 1/2, 32 actual participants, sponsor Inovio Pharmaceuticals, start 2020-10-07 (actual), primary completion 2022-12-15 (actual), last update posted 2026-04-14. Registry predates the approval; noted in the item.
- **Scope:** VOCERYQ appears on FDA's approved cellular and gene therapy products list, content current as of 10/30/2026 — the scope oracle that places this product inside the bulletin.
- Company-stated timelines (order/ship in ~60 days, full field deployment Q1 2027, Syneos Health engagement) are labelled as company statements.

### 3. Karolinska minicircle DNA restores frataxin in Friedreich's ataxia models (preclinical)

- **Primary source:** [Mozafari et al., "In vivo frataxin expression of a triplex purified minicircle," *Gene Therapy*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41434-026-00653-x) — HTTP 200, open access, accepted 7 October 2026, published 31 October 2026. Affiliations: Karolinska Institutet / Karolinska University Hospital (Laboratory Medicine, Karolinska ATMP Center), UT Southwestern O'Donnell Brain Institute.
- Purified minicircle DNA expressing FXN restored frataxin protein to healthy-cell levels in FRDA patient-derived fibroblasts; a single hydrodynamic tail-vein injection produced an 18-fold (liver) and 3-fold (heart) frataxin increase in an FRDA mouse model. Purification uses biotin-conjugated triplex-forming and strand-invading oligonucleotides with a 21 bp H-DNA-forming sequence, complexes removed on streptavidin magnetic beads.
- Framed explicitly as preclinical (fibroblast and mouse work, no human administration). Competing-interest and funding disclosures carried in the item (Rula Zain holds a granted FRDA patent; Hjärnfonden, Swedish Research Council, Swelife-Vinnova, CIMED, Swedish Foundation for Strategic Research, Region Stockholm).
- **Registry sweep:** Friedreich ataxia condition query returns 115 records; five carry gene-therapy interventions — NCT05445323 (Lexeo LX2006, active not recruiting), NCT07721025 (Lexeo LX2006, recruiting Phase 2), NCT05302271 (Weill Cornell AAVrh.10hFXN, recruiting Phase 1), NCT07180355 (Solid SGT-212, recruiting Phase 1), NCT06483802 (Astellas ASP2016, withdrawn). The item states this is a new modality, not a new target.
