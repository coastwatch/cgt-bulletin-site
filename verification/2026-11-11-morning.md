## Notes from this cycle

- **A hold reason is a publishable claim, and it has to be re-tested.** Last cycle held the Autolus item because the poster numbers were "not readable" and the Autolus IR was 502. The company release carries the poster summary in full. The lesson is the one already recorded in the Nov. 10 ledger, now with a second instance: the mirror's page date (Nov. 11) was treated as the release date, when the release body carries a **Nov. 10, 2026** dateline. Both the Autolus hold and the Metagenomi deferral are corrected here.
- **"First" claims get a predicate and a bounded count, not a paraphrase.** IASO's "first lentiviral-vector-based in vivo CAR-T from China to receive an FDA IND clearance" is reproduced as the company's claim, and the six-record sweep is reported with its predicate and its limit: it shows other China-based programs exist and cannot speak to clearance chronology.
- **An absent registry record is a fact worth stating.** Metagenomi's MGX-001 has no ClinicalTrials.gov record in three separate searches, so the published item says so rather than implying an unstated trial.
- **A company enrollment claim and a registry record can disagree, and both get printed.** Humacyte says the CTEV study is open for enrollment; the registry says not yet recruiting with zero sites and a September 2026 start. The item carries both.
- **Read the company's release, not only the newsroom release.** The CTX310 item was published last cycle from the Cleveland Clinic newsroom alone and was therefore missing the 90-day durability signal, the secondary-lipid numbers, the pancreatitis statement and the resolved Grade 2 infusion reaction. The published item now carries the company's own figures and cites the company release.
- **Check a cited publication's date, not just its DOI.** The Nov. 2 item carried the correct PMID and doi but the wrong publication date for the NEJM paper — and the site's own ledger had the date right, so the prose had drifted from its source. The correction is explicit in the published text rather than silent.
- Every published link was HTTP-checked before commit (81 new or touched URLs, all 200 or 203); pages passed tag-balance, duplicate-id, placeholder-link and bare-ampersand checks against a pre-edit backup; the per-note totals ownership assertion passed on all five pages.
