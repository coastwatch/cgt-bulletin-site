### 1. First-in-human in vivo genome editing for Duchenne muscular dystrophy — molecular proof of concept and a fatal adverse event

- **Primary source:** [Mozafari/Li et al., "In vivo genome-editing therapy for Duchenne muscular dystrophy: Preclinical studies and first-in-human trial," *Cell*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.09.059), published online October 26, 2026 — HTTP 200 via the DOI; [PubMed abstract PMID 42933389](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42933389/) — HTTP 200. Publisher pages (cell.com, ScienceDirect) return 403 to this environment, so the DOI and PubMed links are used as the citable targets. Authors confirmed independently via Crossref (22 authors) and PubMed.
- HG302: single AAV9 vector carrying an hfCas12Max editor. Humanized DMD mice: targeted genome editing, partial dystrophin restoration, improved muscle function. Non-human primates: efficient on-target editing, no dose-limiting toxicity under the conditions tested.
- **Human result, stated with its primary failure:** in open-label dose-escalation NCT06594094, post-treatment muscle biopsies gave early molecular evidence of hfCas12Max-mediated editing and detectable dystrophin expression — but at 1.0 × 10¹⁴ vg/kg one participant had a **treatment-related fatal serious adverse event** with marked complement activation and systemic cytokine response shortly after dosing. Authors' own framing: initial human proof-of-concept that also highlights the narrow therapeutic window of systemic AAV-CRISPR therapies.
- **Registry:** NCT06594094 (MUSCLE, study ID HG30201) — early Phase 1, investigator-initiated, sponsor HuidaGene Therapeutics, actual enrollment 4, actual start 2024-11-06, estimated primary completion 2026-08-02, last update posted 2026-08-03, single site at Shanghai Children's Medical Center Affiliated to Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. No posted results (`hasResults: false`).
- **Disclosure arc, in order:** (1) first China gene-editing death — a child in the VGB-Ex01 CRISPR-Cas12b edited HSC trial at the Institute of Hematology and Blood Diseases Hospital, Tianjin (NCT06041620, estimated enrollment 2, last updated 2023-10-17, status still UNKNOWN), reported July 2026, with criticism that the death was never made public; (2) HuidaGene's own second death surfaced August 2026 after a reported long delay; (3) Nature piece on the two Chinese gene-therapy deaths, September 2026; (4) SCMP, October 28, 2026: two children improved, one died, one withdrew. **What is new is the company's own peer-reviewed account of the fatal event.** The item reports a fatal event in a four-participant early Phase 1, not a rate.
- **Duplicate check:** no prior site coverage of HuidaGene, HG302, NCT06594094, hfCas12Max or in vivo DMD editing. The site's existing "in vivo CAR-T in China" item is a Nature Reviews Bioengineering review of CAR-T translation — a different modality and a different paper.

### 2. Brain-sensing synNotch CAR T cells in diffuse midline glioma — durable clearance in mice, fatal neurotoxicity with ICV delivery

- **Primary source:** [Lakshmanachetty et al., "Brain-sensing synNotch–CAR T cells enable precise targeting and clearance of diffuse midline glioma in multiple preclinical models," *Science Translational Medicine* 18(873)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42950017/), published October 28, 2026 — HTTP 200; DOI 10.1126/scitranslmed.aed9994 (science.org DOI link returns 403 here, so the PubMed record is the linked target).
- Mechanism: synNotch receptor recognising brain-restricted brevican (BCAN) gates CAR expression against EphA2 or IL-13Rα2 ("B-SYNC"). Immunodeficient DMG models: single IV or ICV infusion gave tumour infiltration, sustained persistence, antitumour activity and extended survival; immunocompetent syngeneic model with murinised BCAN-gated B7-H3 CAR achieved durable clearance and rechallenge resistance with no detectable neurotoxicity.
- **Safety finding carried explicitly:** ICV administration triggered fatal neurotoxicity because of low-level B7-H3 expression in non-tumour CNS regions — rapid CNS exposure via that route amplified off-tumour toxicity — whereas IV delivery did not. Mechanism: reduced exhaustion, enhanced activation, enrichment of naïve and stem-cell-memory-like subsets.
- **Registry anchor:** NCT06186401 — recruiting Phase 1 of autologous E-SYNC T cells (anti-EGFRvIII synNotch induced anti-EphA2/IL-13Rα2 CAR), sponsor Hideho Okada with CIRM and NCI as collaborators, estimated enrollment 20, single site UCSF, actual start 2024-04-30, estimated primary completion 2027-12-31, last update posted 2026-08-13. The item states that the B-SYNC/DMG data are published separately from that record.
- Framed as preclinical; no IND and no human dosing.
