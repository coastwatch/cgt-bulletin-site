>

Two errors: the record count (19) was wrong, and the consequent "only two active" conclusion was wrong.

### The locked predicate

Run against the ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API. The query `query.cond=Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy&pageSize=1000` returns **502** records (`totalCount=502`, one page, no pagination token). Those 502 are filtered locally.

**Predicate (stated in full, because the published count was wrong):** a record qualifies if

1. `protocolSection.designModule.studyType == "INTERVENTIONAL"`, **and**
2. its `conditionsModule.conditions` matches `Duchenn?e|\bDMD\b`, **and**
3. at least one entry in `armsInterventionsModule.interventions` is a gene-therapy *product*, defined as: intervention `type == "GENETIC"`, **or** the intervention name/description matches `\baav\b|rAAV|adeno-?associated|recombinant \w* vector|vector[- ]based|micro-?dystrophin|minicircle|lentiviral|gene (therapy|transfer|addition)|transgene|follistatin344|GALGT2|SGT-00[123]|RGX-\d|SRP-900|GNT0004|PF-06939926|INS1201`.

**Result: 31 records.** Phase 3-capable (`PHASE3` or `PHASE2`/`PHASE3`): **8**. Active (`RECRUITING`, `ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING`, `NOT_YET_RECRUITING`, `ENROLLING_BY_INVITATION`): **18**. Active **and** Phase 3-capable: **6**, across **5** distinct assets.

### The specific false statement

The published text said "Pfizer's fordadistrogene movaparvovec and Sarepta's delandistrogene follow-up records are terminated." That is wrong in two ways:

- `NCT05689164` is a **long-term follow-up** record, not the Phase 3. Pfizer's Phase 3 is `NCT04281485`, which is **ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING**. The claim that Pfizer's fordadistrogene was terminated was false.
- Sarepta's `NCT05967351` (the follow-up) is **ENROLLING_BY_INVITATION** — not terminated.

### Predicate traps encountered and resolved

Four successive predicates were run before this one; each was rejected because it was wrong in a way that would have published a bad number:

1. `studyType` read from `statusModule` instead of `designModule` → returned 0 records.
2. Keyword scan on intervention *description* only, missing `type == "GENETIC"` records whose descriptions carry no "gene therapy" string → missed `NCT05881408` and `NCT04281485`, giving 18 instead of 31.
3. A loose "dystrophin" token matched **exon-skipping antisense drugs** (e.g. `NCT04179409`, AMONDYS 45 / EXONDYS 51 / VYONDYS 53) → false positives; the "dystrophin gene" phrase was dropped from the product regex.
4. A condition regex `duche+ne` collapsed the set to 7 records. The registry spells Genethon's condition **"Duchene Muscular Dystrophy"** (single n) — a registry typo. Corrected to `Duchenn?e`, restoring `NCT07856329`.

Sanity checks against the locked set: `NCT04179409` (exon skipping) excluded ✅, `NCT01519349` (Becker muscular dystrophy / sporadic inclusion body myositis, non-DMD condition) excluded ✅, `NCT07856329` (Genethon) included ✅.

### The retracted work
"Engineered outer membrane vesicles enhance solid tumour CAR-T cell therapy." Nature Biomedical Engineering 2026;10(4):660–675, doi 10.1038/s41551-025-01575-6, PMID 41501251. Published online 7 January 2026 (EuropePMC `firstPublicationDate` 2026-01-07). Authors: Xianjun Li, Xuehan Li, Jiaqi Shi, Yingjing Li, Hanyu Zhang, Tianjun Chen, Hui Pang, Shuyuan Zhang, Shengnan Luo, Fengyi Liu, Shuang Li, Chujie Ding, Linlin Sun, Fan Xing, Tongsen Zheng — Harbin Medical University Cancer Hospital and Heilongjiang Cancer Institute, Harbin, China. ### The editors' stated reason (verbatim substance)
The editors retracted the article **at the authors' request**. After publication, concerns were raised about the data underlying several of the figures. The source data for **Figures 5 and 6** as well as **Supplementary Figures S10b, S10c, S10f, S10g, S12a and S14b** exhibit unusual numerical patterns where some blocks of observations appear to be mathematically related to other blocks of measurements that should be independent. The authors conducted their own review but were unable to fully account for these issues or provide verifiable copies of the original experimental data. The editors have therefore lost confidence in the integrity of this research. **All authors agree with this retraction.**

## 5. What is not verified

- The BROAD-CAR retraction reason is the editors' and authors' statement as published in the note; the underlying source data and figures could not be independently inspected.
- Whether the separate Sichuan University OMV paper (PMID 42337606) is itself free of the concerns that affected BROAD-CAR is **not** established — it is a different group and platform and is not retracted, and it is published as such.
- The Genethon pivotal-clearance rationale, the 64-boy enrollment figure, the dose-selection rationale, and the MHRA/EMA clearance dates are **company statements** from a July 2025 release, not registry or regulator records.
- The Genethon ESGCT 2026 presentation is **scheduled**, not reported. No GNT0004 safety or efficacy data are published.
- The registry's 77 estimated participants and 14-site footprint do not reconcile with the July 2025 release's 64 boys in France and the UK. This is stated, not resolved.
- The Duchenne sweep predicate is a local filter over 502 returned records, not a server-side query. It is stated in full above so the count of 31 can be reproduced or challenged.
- Genethon's trial record for a study started in 2021 was first posted only on October 2, 2026; whether earlier registry versions existed under a different number was not investigated.
