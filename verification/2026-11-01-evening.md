### Iovance: biomarker-enriched subgroup in second-line lung cancer responds at 59.1% versus 24.6% overall; POSITIVITY cohort launched

- **Primary source:** [Iovance press release, October 29, 2026, filed as Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1425205/000110465926120261/tm2628468d1_ex99-1.htm) — HTTP 200, body read directly; the [8-K itself](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1425205/000110465926120261/tm2628468d1_8k.htm) is Item 8.01, also HTTP 200. Iovance's own IR site returned 403, so the SEC-filed release is the linked primary.
- IOV-LUN-202 Cohorts 1 and 2, second-line non-squamous mNSCLC after platinum chemotherapy and checkpoint inhibition, **no prospective biomarker selection**: 17 of 69 responded (**24.6% ORR**; 15 confirmed responses plus two unconfirmed partial responses awaiting confirmation) at an October 14, 2026 data cutoff — consistent with the previously reported 25.6% in 39 patients.
- **Retrospective** enrichment analysis, step by step: 24.6% overall (17/69) → KRAS wild-type 43.3% (13/30) → KRAS mutant 11.4% (4/35) → + HER2 wild-type 52.0% (13/25) → + current/former smoking 59.1% (13/22). The enriched subgroup was 31.9% of the population but 76.5% of observed responses, and included both complete responses. Four KRAS-unassessed patients excluded; the release notes one uPR pending confirmation in each of the KRAS groups.
- **New development:** POSITIVITY, a first-in-class prospective biomarker-enriched cohort within IOV-LUN-202, enrollment to begin November 2026 at the first of over 70 existing sites, intended to support a potential registrational pathway **subject to FDA feedback**. Separately, frontline Cohort 3A of IOV-COM-202: six of seven patients with non-squamous mNSCLC, KRAS wild-type and a smoking history responded to lifileucel plus pembrolizumab without chemotherapy (85.7% ORR; HER2 status not assessed). OPTILISM, a frontline biomarker-enriched trial, planned for 2027. Company estimates the enriched population at ~45% of US non-squamous mNSCLC cases and more than 90,000 annual deaths.
- **Caveats carried in the item, from the release itself:** the 59.1% is a retrospective subgroup of a single-arm study, two responses are unconfirmed, independent radiology review is ongoing, and the release states that retrospective subgroup findings may not be confirmed in final analyses.
- **Registry:** IOV-LUN-202 = NCT04614103, recruiting Phase 2, 170 estimated, start 2021-05-07, estimated primary completion 2030-12, last update posted 2026-06-26. IOV-COM-202 = NCT03645928, Phase 2, 225 estimated, start 2019-05-07, estimated primary completion 2026-02-20, **status TERMINATED**, last update posted 2026-05-15 — noted in the item, because the frontline Cohort 3A data come from a terminated study record.
- **Scope note:** lifileucel is FDA-approved (AMTAGVI) and on FDA's approved cellular and gene therapy products list, so this is a label-expansion and patient-selection story for an approved cell therapy.
- **Duplicate check:** the site has no coverage of IOV-LUN-202, NCT04614103, KRAS, POSITIVITY or POSI TIL. The existing IOV-COM-202 coverage is the October 24 ESMO frontline **melanoma** readout, a different cohort and indication.
