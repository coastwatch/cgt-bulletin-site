### 1. KYSA-8 registrational data for mivocabtagene autoleucel in stiff person syndrome published in JAMA Neurology

- **Primary source:** Piquet AL, Zekeridou A, Gergis U, Gutman J et al. (22 authors), "Mivocabtagene Autoleucel CD19 Chimeric Antigen Receptor T-Cell Therapy in Stiff Person Syndrome," *JAMA Neurology*, published November 9, 2026 (doi 10.1001/jamaneurol.2026.4155) — Crossref-confirmed title, date, journal and authorship; the [article PDF](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/articlepdf/2856388/jamaneurology_piquet_2026_oi_260076_1793718467.6184.pdf) and DOI were HTTP-checked, and the abstract was read in full via the Crossref record.
- **Design:** pivotal, single-arm, open-label, nonrandomized Phase 2 (KYSA-8) at three US sites, enrolling September 2024 to May 2025; participants aged 18–75 with antibody-positive stiff person syndrome and inadequate response to immunomodulatory or immunosuppressive therapies. **27 enrolled, 26 treated** (median age 56, range 32–73; 16 female); median follow-up 6.5 months (range 4.4–12.2). Treatment was **low-dose lymphodepletion** — fludarabine 30 mg/m²/day plus cyclophosphamide 300 mg/m²/day for three days — followed by a single infusion of 1 × 10⁸ CAR T cells.
- **Primary endpoint met:** week-16 improvement from baseline in the timed 25-foot walk of mean **−7.69 seconds (SD 10.03; 95% CI −11.74 to −3.64; P < .001)**, median −4.8 seconds, a **45.6% median improvement**; improvement was sustained (median 44.4%) among the 16 of 26 patients reaching the 24-week timepoint. All four secondary endpoints improved significantly at week 16: modified Rankin Scale mean −0.8 (95% CI −1.1 to −0.4), Distribution of Stiffness Index mean −1.5 (−2.2 to −0.8), Heightened Sensitivity Scale mean −3.2 (−4.0 to −2.3), Hauser Ambulation Index mean −1.6 (−2.1 to −1.2), all P < .001.
- **Safety:** most common grade 3/4 treatment-related adverse event was neutropenia in 5 of 26 (19.2%). Low-grade CRS in 24 of 26 (92.3%; 10 grade 1, 14 grade 2) and ICANS in 3 of 26 (11.5%, all grade 1), **all resolving without sequelae**. B-cell depletion and changes suggestive of immune reset were observed, and **at last follow-up all patients were free of immunomodulatory therapies for stiff person syndrome**.
- **Registry (v2 API; robots.txt read first this session):** NCT06588491 (KYSA-8) — completed Phase 2, 27 participants, sponsor Kyverna Therapeutics, actual start 2024-09-25, estimated primary completion 2026-06-25, last update posted 2026-07-24, three US sites; intervention recorded as standard lymphodepletion with KYV-101. No posted results on the record.
- **Boundaries carried:** a nonrandomized single-arm trial in 26 treated patients with median follow-up of 6.5 months — the durability claim rests on the 16 patients who reached 24 weeks and on long-term follow-up, which the paper states is ongoing. The site carried mivocabtagene previously only as an RMAT-designated programme and a named-patient case; this is the registrational publication.

### 2. Nkarta reports the first human data for a CAR NK-cell therapy in autoimmune disease

- **Primary source:** [Nkarta press release, November 9, 2026, Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1787400/000178740026000027/nktx-ex99_1.htm) — HTTP 200, body read directly; the 8-K primary document (Item 8.01) was also read.
- **Design:** NKX019, an allogeneic, off-the-shelf CD19-directed CAR natural killer cell therapy, in idiopathic inflammatory myopathy and systemic sclerosis, in the Ntrust-2 basket study, presented at ACR Convergence 2026 (two orals, one poster). All data as of a **September 18, 2026** cut-off. Patients at the 4 billion-cell dose level received **three doses on Days 0, 3 and 7** after lymphodepleting preconditioning, for 12 billion cells total.
- **Myositis:** of six patients at the 4 billion-cell dose with at least three months of follow-up, **five (83%) achieved moderate or major Total Improvement Score responses**, spanning three subtypes — dermatomyositis, juvenile dermatomyositis and antisynthetase syndrome; five of six (83%) also improved on Manual Muscle Testing 8, and four of eight evaluable (50%) achieved clinically relevant reductions in skin disease activity on the Cutaneous Dermatomyositis Disease Area and Severity Index.
- **Systemic sclerosis:** **six of seven (86%) achieved rCRISS-25 or greater and five of seven (71%) rCRISS-50 or greater**, with a median 15-point reduction in modified Rodnan skin score at Day 90 and five of seven (71%) reaching at least a 50% reduction from baseline.
- **Safety — the finding that matters most for a cell-therapy audience:** across **40 patients treated across five autoimmune indications and three dose levels**, there were **no cases of CRS of any grade, no ICANS, no IEC-HS, no GvHD and no leukoencephalopathy** (the fludarabine-associated neurotoxicity of record), **no deaths, no treatment discontinuations due to adverse events**, and no increase in adverse events at higher doses. Deep peripheral CD19+ B-cell depletion was observed at the 4 billion-cell dose.
- **Registry (v2 API; robots.txt read first this session):** autoimmune basket NCT06557265 — recruiting Phase 1/2, 120 estimated, sponsor Nkarta, actual start 2024-06-13, estimated primary completion 2027-04, last update posted 2026-06-04, 19 sites across Australia, Puerto Rico and the United States; immune-mediated disease basket NCT06733935 — recruiting Phase 1/2, 240 estimated, actual start 2024-11-04, estimated primary completion 2028-10, last update posted 2026-10-30, 12 US sites; systemic lupus erythematosus study NCT06518668 — enrolling by invitation, 6 estimated, sponsor Hospital for Special Surgery, last update posted 2026-06-22. Oncology study NCT05020678 is active not recruiting, 150 estimated.
- **Boundaries carried:** initial, non-randomised, single-arm Phase 1/2 data at a September 18 cut-off presented at a conference; the myositis and systemic sclerosis figures are six and seven patients at one dose level. The site carried no prior Nkarta or CAR NK autoimmune coverage.

## Notes from this cycle

- **A registrational dataset published in a top-tier journal outranks every re-run of it.** The KYSA-8 result is a pivotal single-arm Phase 2 in 26 treated patients with a met primary endpoint, four secondary endpoints and every patient off immunomodulatory therapy at last follow-up — the site had carried the programme only as an RMAT designation and a named-patient case.
- **Two immune-reset mechanisms in one day, and the contrast is publishable.** mivocabtagene is autologous with low-dose lymphodepletion and CRS in 92% (all grade 1–2) plus grade 1 ICANS in 11.5%; NKX019 is off-the-shelf NK with **no CRS at any grade, no ICANS, no IEC-HS and no GvHD across 40 patients**. The item pairs them so a reader can see that "off-the-shelf" currently buys a different safety profile, not just a different manufacturing model.
