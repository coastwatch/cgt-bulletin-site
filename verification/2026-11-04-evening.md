### 1. Clonal hematopoiesis after CAR19 linked to prolonged cytopenias, impaired immune recovery and treatment-related myeloid malignancy

- **Primary source:** Hamilton MP, Phillips N, Alizadeh AA et al., "Clonal hematopoiesis and cytopenias following CAR19 therapy," *Nature Genetics* (published November 2, 2026) — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-026-02795-5 — HTTP 200, open access, full article body read. A *Nature Genetics* research briefing summarising it is at https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-026-02805-6 (HTTP 200).
- Design: lymphoma patients receiving CD19-directed CAR T cells (axi-cel) compared with propensity-matched autologous HCT (autoHCT) controls, **69 per arm**, LBCL, January 2018–July 2022; capture-based sequencing, flow cytometry, scRNA-seq and scDNA-seq; cfDNA/PBL dataset of **1,123 unique samples from 169 unique patients**, median deduplicated depth 2,809×.
- **Findings as reported:** grade ≥3 infections higher in CAR arm (HR 2.54; 95% CI 1.26–5.41); non-relapse mortality beyond day 30 increased, driven mainly by infection (P = 0.027); delayed immune reconstitution with mean recovery differences per day of WBC −2.08, ANC −0.99 and ALC −0.796 ×10⁹ cells/L. Bone marrow analysis associated prolonged post-CAR cytopenias with clonal cytopenias of undetermined significance and interferon-mediated inflammation (IL-4, PDGF-AA/BB, CCL7 and IL-13 significantly downregulated in cytopenic marrow).
- **Population-level tMN figures:** of **533** post-CAR-treated NHL patients screened at Stanford, **17** developed treatment-related myeloid neoplasm — incidence **4.2% at 3 years**, median time to onset **741 days**. Among patients with ≥90 days of follow-up (n = 458), TP53 mutation raised tMN odds (OR 35.35; 95% CI 3.94–4601.07; P = 3.32 × 10⁻⁴) while DNMT3A mutation was protective (OR 0.085; 95% CI 5.73 × 10⁻⁴–0.95; P = 0.045); multiple cellular therapies before transformation was associated with tMN (OR 3.57; 95% CI 1.33–9.59). Post-CAR tMN was molecularly and clinically comparable to post-autoHCT tMN (29 cases). In one patient with CAR19 followed by CAR22, a TP53 mutation first detected on day 7 expanded after the second infusion, followed by LOH. The CH-mutant fraction of the CAR+ population ranged 0–61.5%, exceeding 20% in multiple patients.
- **Author caveats carried in the item:** most patients with CH expansion do not develop a secondary neoplasm and most expansions are probably clinically inert; at this cohort size pre-infusion CH did not predict subsequent tMN, including restricted to TP53; CH was not associated with ICANS or CRS; it is unclear whether CH causes prolonged cytopenia or is a consequence of impaired haematopoiesis. The authors suggest monitoring CH mutations, particularly TP53, in some post-CAR patients with cytopenia, and state pre-existing CH should not preclude treatment. Stated limitations: pandemic-era COVID-19 exposure, excellent autoHCT outcomes, and potentially elevated tMN risk from CD22-targeting CAR T cells at Stanford.
- **Duplicate check:** the site had no prior coverage of clonal hematopoiesis, CH expansion, TP53-mutated tMN risk, or this study.

### 2. A2 Biotherapeutics: complete responses in NSCLC and anal cancer from logic-gated CAR T programmes

- **Primary source:** [A2 Biotherapeutics release, November 3, 2026](https://www.a2bio.com/a2-biotherapeutics-presents-updated-safety-and-efficacy-data-from-everest-2-and-denali-1-clinical-studies-during-2026-annual-meeting-of-the-society-for-immunotherapy-of-cancer/) — HTTP 200, body read directly (company IR 502 from this environment).
- **EVEREST-2 (NCT06051695), autologous MSLN-directed:** as of September 1, 2026, 16 patients enrolled — 13 on A2B694 (MSLN activator + HLA-A*02 blocker) across four dose levels (1–6 × 10⁸ cells), median age 59; 3 on A2B543 (same construct + NFAT-driven membrane-tethered IL-12 booster) at DL1 (4 × 10⁸ cells), all three women aged 45, 45 and 51 with pancreatic cancer. Tumor types: colorectal n=4, pancreatic n=3, ovarian n=3, NSCLC n=1, gastroesophageal n=1, mesothelioma n=1. Among 13 evaluable Arm 1 patients: CR n=1 (NSCLC), SD n=8, PD n=4; the CR was confirmed at Day 90 by central review per RECIST 1.1 and ongoing beyond 12 months. Arm 2: two evaluable pancreatic patients, both SD. Safety across all 16: no DLTs, no grade 4 Tmod-related AEs; Arm 2 one grade 1 CRS; Arm 1 one grade 3 ICANS, one grade 1 ICANS, one grade 1 CRS. Serum IL-12 remained <5 pg/mL through Day 56 in both A2B543 patients; maximum tolerated dose not reached.
- **DENALI-1 (NCT06682793), allogeneic EGFR-directed:** as of August 21, 2026, 11 patients received A2B395 (anal n=1, head and neck n=1, NSCLC n=2, colorectal n=4, breast n=3; median age 56, range 33–70, 80% women). Anal cancer patient with five prior chemotherapy regimens and two courses of radiotherapy: CR on Day 28, resolution of all target and non-target lesions, no serious AEs. Head and neck cancer patient with multiple prior therapies: PR on Day 180, ongoing disease control at 6 months. Among nine efficacy-evaluable patients: CR n=1, PR n=1, SD n=5, PD n=2 — **disease control rate 78%**. No DLTs, no GVHD, no IEC-HS; one grade 1 CRS and one grade 2, both resolved.
- **Registry (v2 API, robots.txt read first this session):** NCT06051695 recruiting Phase 1/2, 474 estimated, A2 Biotherapeutics, start 2024-04-03, estimated primary completion 2028-06, last update posted 2026-06-12, 12 sites, United States. NCT06682793 recruiting Phase 1/2, 240 estimated, start 2025-05-22, estimated primary completion 2029-03-31, last update posted 2025-09-09, 10 sites, United States. Patient prescreening occurs through BASECAMP-1 (NCT04981119, recruiting, 500 estimated, last update 2026-10-26).
- **Framing:** the "first reported complete response in lung cancer" and "first reported CR in metastatic anal cancer" claims are attributed to the company; the registry records carry no posted efficacy results; both studies are early-phase, open-label, single-arm with 9 and 13 evaluable patients, presented at SITC (Nov 4–8, 2026) rather than published in a peer-reviewed journal.

NCT06743503 — exploratory Phase 1 of UB-VV400 with rapamycin, 70 estimated, sponsor Nanjing IASO Biotechnology, last update 2025-09-18.
