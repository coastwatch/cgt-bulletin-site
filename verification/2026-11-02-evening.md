### 1. FDA grants RMAT designations to CTX310 (CRISPR Therapeutics, in vivo ANGPTL3 editing)

- **Primary source:** [CRISPR Therapeutics Q3 2026 business update, November 2, 2026, filed as Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1674416/000119312526443644/crsp-ex99_1.htm) — HTTP 200, body read directly.
- FDA granted **RMAT designations to CTX310 for severe hypertriglyceridemia (sHTG) and for patients with elevated LDL-C**. RMAT confers the benefits of Fast Track and Breakthrough Therapy, including early FDA interaction and potential eligibility for priority review and accelerated approval.
- **Peer-reviewed support, linked and cited:** *New England Journal of Medicine* — "Phase 1 Trial of CRISPR-Cas9 Gene Editing Targeting ANGPTL3" (PMID 41211945, doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2511778, published November 27, 2025) and the durability letter "Durability of CRISPR-Cas9 Gene Editing Targeting ANGPTL3 with CTX310" (PMID 42663980, doi 10.1056/NEJMc2609825, October 1, 2026). Company-reported figures: a single course of treatment produced reductions sustained through one year; at the highest dose mean reductions from baseline were **79% ANGPTL3, 48% triglycerides, 53% LDL-C** in a mixed cohort (high triglycerides, high LDL-C, or both); well tolerated with no treatment-related serious adverse events.
- **Registry:** NCT07491172 (CRSP-CVD-400) — recruiting Phase 1, first-in-human ascending-dose trial in refractory dyslipidemias, 90 estimated participants, sponsor CRISPR Therapeutics AG, actual start 2024-06-21, estimated primary completion 2027-06, last update posted 2026-09-18, first posted 2026-03-24. The record carries **no locations listed** as of that update — noted.
- **Phase 1b data are scheduled, not reported:** a late-breaking oral presentation at AHA Scientific Sessions on November 9, 2026. Labelled as scheduled in the item.
- **Also disclosed:** CTX611's Phase 2 trial in total knee arthroplasty has **completed enrollment**, and zugo-cel Phase 1 data in systemic sclerosis are to be presented at ACR Convergence 2026. A registry sweep returns **no CTX611 record and no zugo-cel record** — flagged in the item rather than left implicit.
- **Commercial and platform context from the same release:** CASGEVY generated **$99 million in Q3 2026 revenue, up 30% QoQ and nearly six times the $17 million of Q3 2025**, with more than 750 patients treated worldwide and twice as many patients treated in the first nine months of 2026 as in all of 2025. CRISPR Therapeutics also disclosed an **in vivo haematopoietic stem cell editing approach on LNP delivery**, designed to edit stem cells in the body and broaden access beyond the ex vivo Casgevy workflow — preclinical, no registry record.

### 2. Vertex reports CASGEVY Q3 2026 revenue of $99.2 million, plus UAE, Bahrain and Saudi regulatory progress

- **Primary source:** [Vertex Pharmaceuticals third quarter 2026 results, November 2, 2026, Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/875320/000087532026000287/ex-991_q32026.htm) — HTTP 200, body read directly.
- **CASGEVY revenue $99.2 million in Q3 2026**, against **$16.9 million in Q3 2025** and **$218.5 million for the first nine months of 2026**; company describes 30% quarter-over-quarter growth.
- **Regulatory state confirmed from the filing:** approved for patients 12 and older in **36 countries**; for patients **2 and older in the United States, Bahrain and the United Arab Emirates**; and for patients **5 and older in the United Kingdom** following the MHRA extension published October 30. **New in this filing:** the UAE and Bahrain approved CASGEVY for children aged 2 and older in October, and Vertex has **completed regulatory submissions in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia**. Multiple children have initiated treatment in the US since the July FDA approval for ages 2+.
- **Guidance:** FY2026 total revenue raised to $13.15–13.25 billion; embedded expectations of **$500 million or more in combined CASGEVY and JOURNAVX revenue**, unchanged.
- **Framing:** growth is real and accelerating from a small base, but $99 million a quarter against a multi-billion-dollar launch plan for an autologous, myeloablative, lymphodepleting product with one manufacturing slot per patient is an early trajectory. The item states plainly that the site's earlier "sales lagging" framing needs updating against this figure, and that the per-patient operational burden is unchanged while the pediatric expansions widen the funnel.
