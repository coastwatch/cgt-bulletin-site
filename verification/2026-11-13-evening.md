## Published

### 1. Obsidian reports updated amsoki-cel data in advanced melanoma

- **Primary source:** [SEC EDGAR — Obsidian 8-K Exhibit 99.1, November 12, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130606/000119312526458347/d20599dex991.htm) — HTTP 200 with a declared User-Agent, body read directly.
- **Data presented at the 41st Annual SITC Meeting:** updated efficacy and pooled safety data for **amsoki-cel (formerly OBX-115)**, Obsidian's engineered tumour-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) cell therapy, in advanced melanoma, including a **67% objective response rate (n=10 of 15)**, **median progression-free survival of 8.3 months**, and **median duration of response not yet reached at 14.3 months median study follow-up**. The item leads with the denominator — 10 responses in 15 patients is a 15-patient dataset, not a registrational one.
- **Programme status:** enrolment is ongoing in the **registration-enabling cohort of the Phase 1/2 Agni-01 multicentre study**, with **topline data expected by year-end 2027**.
- **Mechanism:** Obsidian engineers TIL cells using its protein-regulation technology, described by the company as regulating proteins rather than manipulating gene expression.
- **Registry (v2 API; robots.txt read first this session):** a sponsor sweep returns **one** record — **NCT06060613**, "Safety and Efficacy of OBX-115 in Advanced Solid Tumors", **recruiting** Phase 1/2, **254 participants estimated**, sponsor Obsidian Therapeutics, Inc., actual start October 25, 2023, estimated primary completion June 30, 2029, last update posted November 3, 2026, **14 sites across the United States and Australia**. **Two things the item flags:** the 254 estimate covers the whole Phase 1/2 programme across advanced solid tumours, while the SITC data are the 15-patient melanoma subset; and the record still carries the **pre-rename OBX-115** designation.
- **Financials:** cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of **$324.4 million** at September 30, 2026, expected cash runway into 2H 2028. Mark Reisenauer appointed to the Board.

### 2. MiNK's off-the-shelf iNKT therapy earns first product revenue, and ARPA-H picks it for room-temperature deployment

- **Primary source:** [SEC EDGAR — MiNK 8-K Exhibit 99.1, November 12, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1840229/000119312526457244/inkt-ex99_1.htm) — HTTP 200 with a declared User-Agent, body read directly. The release itself is datelined **NEW YORK, Nov. 11, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE)**.
- **Revenue:** agenT-797, an allogeneic invariant natural killer T (iNKT) cell therapy, has **generated its first product revenue** through physician-requested named-patient access for patients with serious unmet medical need.
- **The clinical programme:** a **randomised Phase 2 trial in acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) is enrolling**, with **US dosing expected before year-end**. The population is described by the company as having **~40% 28-day mortality, no effective approved therapies, and an estimated 93,000 US patients annually**.
- **Registry (v2 API):** **NCT07615010** is a **recruiting** Phase 2 adaptive randomised placebo-controlled trial of agenT-797 plus standard of care versus placebo plus standard of care in severe pneumonia with moderate-to-severe acute hypoxemic respiratory failure, **90 participants estimated**, sponsor MiNK Therapeutics, actual start May 26, 2026, estimated primary completion June 2027, estimated completion August 2027, **last update posted June 26, 2026**, **four sites across the United States and Ukraine**. **The site count and the country list are the item's point:** a four-site trial, one of them in Ukraine, bears directly on how much weight a "US dosing expected before year-end" milestone can carry. The record has also not been updated since June 26, 2026.
- **The federal programme:** MiNK was **selected as the cell therapy for the Draper-led team in ARPA-H's BoSS programme** — **up to $87 million in programme-wide funding** — to move living medicines toward **room-temperature deployability**. **This is a manufacturing programme, not a clinical one**, and it is the most consequential thing in the release for a manufacturing reader: an off-the-shelf iNKT product that could be shipped and held rather than manufactured per patient.
- **Also at SITC 2026:** agenT-797 and MiNK-215 acted as **"living senolytics,"** clearing senescent cells that chemotherapy leaves behind, which the company says fuel treatment resistance and fibrotic lung disease; and agenT-797 unlocked tumour-cell killing with BOT/BAL in liver-metastatic colorectal-cancer organoids.
- **Financials:** 23% year-over-year improvement in net loss per share, a 44% G&A efficiency gain, described as the most capital-efficient quarter of 2026.
- **Duplicate check:** MiNK appeared on these pages only in a Nov. 7 held-candidate line, with no item; a grep for "agenT-797" across all five pages returns **zero** hits before this cycle.

### 3. Benitec's BB-301 shows a 100% responder rate in OPMD — the figure its October release put at 67%

- **Primary source:** [SEC EDGAR — Benitec 8-K Exhibit 99.1, November 13, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1808898/000119312526459339/bntc-ex99_1.htm) — HTTP 200 with a declared User-Agent, body read directly. The 8-K is Item 2.02 plus **Item 5.03** (a fiscal-year change).
- **The headline and its predecessor, both published:** Benitec reported interim clinical results from the BB-301 Phase 1b/2a study in dysphagic patients with oculopharyngeal muscular dystrophy (OPMD) at the ESGCT Annual Congress, showing a **100% responder rate across all Cohort 1 (low dose) and Cohort 2 (high dose) patients**, with the **pivotal study design to be finalised in the first calendar quarter of 2027** and the company **on track to begin the pivotal trial in mid-2027**. **The item gives the predecessor too:** the company's own **October 28, 2026** release of the same poster reported that **67% of patients in Cohort 1 and Cohort 2 met the statistical criteria for response at the 3-month post-treatment time-point**. The 100% figure is therefore not a change in the data but a change in framing, and **the release does not state how the two are reconciled** — the October number was a time-point-specific statistical criterion and this one is a responder rate across cohorts. Publishing 100% alone would have been misleading.
- **Safety:** **there have been no Serious Adverse Events in any patient treated with BB-301 to date.**
- **What was presented:** interim results for the **three patients in Cohort 2** and full 12-month results for the **six patients in Cohort 1**.
- **The product and the manufacturing approach:** BB-301 is a **modified AAV9 capsid** expressing a single bifunctional construct that co-expresses codon-optimised **PABPN1** with **two siRNAs against mutant PABPN1**, the causative gene for OPMD. The siRNAs are modelled into microRNA backbones to silence the faulty mutant transcript while allowing expression of the codon-optimised protein to replace it — the company's "Silence and Replace" ddRNAi platform. It is administered **locally, in a one-time procedure, to the muscles in the throat that drive swallowing**, which the company says is designed to maximise local benefit and minimise systemic exposure.
- **Registry (v2 API):** **NCT06185673** is a **recruiting** Phase 1/2 study of intramuscular BB-301 in OPMD, **30 participants estimated**, sponsor Benitec Biopharma, Inc., actual start November 28, 2023, estimated primary completion November 2030, estimated completion November 2040, **last update posted December 31, 2025**, **one US site**, with four intervention arms — dose-escalation Phase 1b cohorts 1, 2 and 3 and a dose-expansion Phase 2a. **Two things the item flags:** the registry record was last updated in December 2025 while the company reports nine patients dosed across two cohorts; and a pivotal study starting mid-2027 rests on a base of nine treated patients.
- **On the company's "first and only" framing:** Benitec describes BB-301 as "the first and only disease-modifying genetic medicine in clinical development" for OPMD. A registry sweep of OPMD-condition records returns **16 studies**, of which the other genetic-medicine programmes are **myoblast transplantation trials — two autologous myoblast studies in OPMD dysphagia (one a completed Phase 2/3 in France with 30 participants, one a terminated Phase 2) — plus Cabaletta's CABA-201, a completed Phase 2 in 25 participants, and a Phase 2 trehalose study that was withdrawn**. Those are cell-based and genetic approaches to the same disease, so the sweep does not support "only." The claim is attributed to the company and not adopted.
- **Financials:** total expenses $14.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 against $9.8 million a year earlier; R&D $8.6 million against $3.4 million; G&A $5.9 million against $6.4 million; net loss $12.9 million, or $(0.26) per share, against $9.0 million and $(0.22); **$176.5 million in cash and cash equivalents**. Benitec also announced a **change of fiscal year-end from June 30 to December 31**, with operational and programme guidance now on a calendar-year basis.
- **Duplicate check:** the site had **zero** prior mentions of Benitec, BB-301, OPMD, PABPN1 or ddRNAi on any of the five pages, and no ledger mentions them.

## Notes from this cycle

- **EDGAR full-text search is a reliable way to sweep an entire evening window.** `https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q="cell therapy"&forms=8-K&startdt=2026-11-12&enddt=2026-11-13` returned 25 filings, which mapped to 17 issuers after de-duplication, and three of them turned out to carry genuine clinical or regulatory news that no news-wire search surfaced. This is how Obsidian, MiNK and Benitec were found. `web_search` remains unavailable, so this is the substitute.
- **A company's own earlier release is a primary source for its own later number.** Benitec's 100% responder rate and its own October 67% both come from the same poster, and the current release does not reconcile them. The October release was fetched and quoted, and the item carries both figures with the explanation that the criteria differ — publishing 100% alone would have been a misleading number, not merely an incomplete one.
- **A registry record can undercut a milestone in the same release.** MiNK says US dosing is expected before year-end; the registry lists four sites, one of them in Ukraine, and has not been updated since June. Both are in the item.
- **A programme's own naming can lag its registry record.** NCT06060613 still reads OBX-115 after the rename to amsoki-cel, and NCT06185673's estimated completion runs to 2040 for a trial whose pivotal study starts in 2027.
- **"First and only" needs a sweep of the same disease, not the same modality.** Benitec's OPMD claim fails against autologous myoblast transplantation trials and Cabaletta's CABA-201 — all in the same disease, all registered, none a AAV gene therapy. The sweep is reported with its bounded result and the claim stays attributed.
- **Three items is a good evening.** The window contained three genuine, primary-verified clinical developments; everything else was already covered, out of scope, pre-cutoff, or without a primary.
- Every published link was checked before commit (all 200 with the appropriate User-Agent); pages passed tag-balance, duplicate-id, placeholder-link and bare-ampersand checks against a pre-edit baseline; the per-note totals ownership assertion passed on all five pages, and each historical note retains its own counts.
