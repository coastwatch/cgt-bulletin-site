### 2. Nature Cancer: radioinducible CAR T-cells release TNF only where the tumour is irradiated

- **Primary source:** Masoumi E, Walczak P, Tsuji T et al., "Spatiotemporal control of CAR-T cell TNF release via tumor-targeted irradiation," *Nature Cancer*, published November 5, 2026 (https://www.nature.com/articles/s43018-026-01255-7, DOI 10.1038/s43018-026-01255-7) — HTTP 200, abstract and figure captions read. Subscription content beyond the abstract; the item is bounded to what the abstract states.
- **Mechanism:** the authors identified radiation-responsive genes in tumor-infiltrating T cells and engineered **synthetic radioinducible promoters** (the DD56×9 construct, derived from the mouse Phlda3 promoter) to drive TNF expression. Human CAR T-cells carrying it (riTNF CAR-T cells) secreted high levels of TNF upon irradiation **independently of antigen engagement** — which is the mechanism that makes the spatial control work, since it does not depend on the tumour being antigen-positive.
- **Results as reported:** in human xenograft and murine tumor models, riTNF CAR-T cells plus local irradiation produced **greater tumor regression than either treatment alone, or standard CAR T-cells with irradiation**. The response was associated with high **intratumoral but not systemic** TNF levels, increased tumor necrosis and apoptosis, vascular disruption and greater CAR T-cell infiltration.
- **Limitation the authors report:** maximal efficacy **required irradiation of all tumor sites**, despite macrophage-associated abscopal responses after single-lesion irradiation.
- **Scope:** preclinical — human xenograft and mouse tumor models, no human administration; data deposited in GEO under GSE304882 and GSE305175. A registry sweep returns no registered study of irradiation-controlled cytokine release, so nothing is in the clinic.
- **Manufacturing read carried in the item:** this is a different design philosophy from the in vivo editing programmes. The payload is not edited into the genome but switched on by an external signal, so the manufactured product stays constant and control moves to the treatment protocol. For a CDMO that reframes release testing: a fixed cell line with an inducible promoter means lot release measures the construct and a basal state rather than per-patient potency, with tumour-directed activity delivered by radiotherapy scheduling rather than by manufacturing.
