### 1. Cleveland Clinic reports the first Phase 1b data for CTX310, CRISPR Therapeutics' in vivo ANGPTL3 base editor

- **Primary source:** [Cleveland Clinic newsroom release, November 9, 2026](https://newsroom.clevelandclinic.org/2026/11/09/gene-editing-therapy-trial-dramatically-lowers-dangerous-blood-fat-levels) — HTTP 200, body read directly. Presented in a late-breaking science session at the American Heart Association annual meeting in Chicago.
- **Results as reported:** a Phase 1b trial of **CTX310**, an in vivo CRISPR-Cas9 base editing therapy targeting ANGPTL3, in **10 adults with severe hypertriglyceridemia**: 30 days after a single intravenous infusion, triglycerides fell **an average of 63% (reductions up to 85%)**, ANGPTL3 levels dropped **more than 65%**, and non-HDL cholesterol, apolipoprotein B and triglyceride-rich lipoprotein cholesterol were also lowered. Generally well tolerated with **no serious side effects related to the treatment observed**. The study builds on earlier Phase 1 research across lipid disorders and doses and was funded by CRISPR Therapeutics AG. Study presenter: Ashish Sarraju, M.D., Cleveland Clinic.
- **Registry (v2 API; robots.txt read first this session):** NCT07491172 (CRSP-CVD-400) — recruiting Phase 1 open-label, multicenter, first-in-human ascending-dose trial of CTX310 in refractory dyslipidemias, 90 estimated, sponsor CRISPR Therapeutics AG, actual start 2024-06-21, estimated primary completion 2027-06, last update posted 2026-09-18, 17 sites across Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom and the United States.
- **Boundary carried:** this is a 30-day biochemical readout, not a durability result. The release itself says the hope is that the approach could provide long-lasting benefits without ongoing medication and that **additional research is needed to determine how durable the effects may be**. The item says plainly that 30-day response is not proof of a one-time cure in a chronic metabolic disease.
- **Why a separate item rather than an update:** the site's Nov. 2 coverage reported the RMAT designations and one-year durability data from CRISPR Therapeutics' earlier presentation; this is the first disclosure of the Cleveland Clinic Phase 1b dataset — a different study and a different readout.
- **Scope boundary stated in the item:** this concerns CTX310, a gene-editing therapy, **not** the Arrowhead ARO-DIMER-PA small interfering RNA study that this bulletin has held as out of scope in each of the last four cycles.

### 2. AbelZeta wins FDA RMAT and Fast Track for its bispecific CAR T-cell therapy Prizlon-cel

- **Primary source:** [AbelZeta release, November 10, 2026 (PR Newswire)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/abelzeta-receives-fda-rmat-and-fast-track-designations-for-prizlon-cel-in-relapsed-or-refractory-large-b-cell-lymphoma-302934695.html) — HTTP 200, body read directly (Rockville, Md., Nov. 10, 2026).
- FDA granted **both RMAT and Fast Track** to Prizlon-cel (Prizloncabtagene Autoleucel, also known as A-CAR039), an investigational **anti-CD19/CD20 bispecific autologous CAR T-cell therapy**, for relapsed or refractory large B-cell lymphoma, under IND application **STN 32908**.
- **Development strategy, which is the substantive part:** a two-track approach — later-line patients who have already progressed after an FDA-approved CAR T-cell therapy, and patients treated earlier in the disease course. For the first, AbelZeta plans to evaluate Prizlon-cel in **ABZT-121**, a Phase 2 study in r/r LBCL after prior FDA-approved CAR T-cell therapy, and says it **intends to evaluate whether results from ABZT-121 could support a potential accelerated-approval pathway**, subject to clinical results and future discussions with FDA. In parallel it is developing **ABZT-131**, a randomized second-line study.
- **Designation meaning:** RMAT provides intensive FDA interaction on clinical development including discussions intended to support efficient development and potential approval; Fast Track facilitates development and expedites review for serious conditions with unmet need, and may permit more frequent FDA interactions and rolling review of portions of a future BLA if applicable requirements are met. AbelZeta regained all global development, regulatory, manufacturing and commercialization rights relating to Prizlon-cel in July 2026, and received FDA clearance of the IND in August 2026.
- **Registry (v2 API; robots.txt read first this session):** NCT07875413 (ABZT-121) — **not yet recruiting** Phase 2 multi-center study of an autologous anti-CD19/CD20 CAR T-cell therapy in r/r LBCL after prior FDA-approved CAR T-cell therapy, 55 estimated, sponsor AbelZeta, estimated start 2027-05, estimated primary completion 2029-06, last update posted 2026-10-15, two US sites. **ABZT-131 was not located** in this sweep.
- **Boundaries carried:** the accelerated-approval pathway is the company's stated intention and is explicitly conditional on clinical results and future discussions with FDA; designations are not approvals; the registry record describes a study that has not begun enrolling, with an estimated start in 2027.
