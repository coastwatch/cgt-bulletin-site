## Published

### Intellia Therapeutics: lonvo-z has a March 10, 2027 PDUFA date — the first potential approval of an in vivo CRISPR therapy

- **Primary source:** Intellia Q3 2026 results and business update, November 5, 2026 — filed as Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K, read in full at https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652130/000119312526448673/ntla-ex99_1.htm (HTTP 200, with a declared User-Agent as SEC requires).
- **Regulatory:** lonvoguran ziclumeran (lonvo-z), an in vivo CRISPR therapy for hereditary angioedema, has a **PDUFA target action date of March 10, 2027**, following FDA acceptance of the BLA and Priority Review in September 2026. The company states FDA **advised it is not currently planning to hold an advisory committee** to discuss the application — a direct contrast with the advisory committee it tentatively signalled for uniQure's Huntington's therapy three days earlier. If approved, lonvo-z would be the first in vivo CRISPR-based therapy and the only one-time treatment for HAE.
- **Mechanism and model of use:** a one-time outpatient treatment designed to inactivate the **KLKB1** gene to permanently lower kallikrein and bradykinin levels and eliminate attacks and lifelong treatment.
- **Commercial readiness:** the field organisation deployment is complete, with engagement deepening across HAE treatment centres and payers for a planned Q1 2027 launch.
- **Second programme:** nexiguran ziclumeran (nex-z), in vivo CRISPR targeting the liver to inactivate TTR, partnered with **Regeneron**, completed enrolment in the Phase 3 MAGNITUDE-2 study in ATTRv-PN **exceeding its targeted randomisation of 60 patients**, advanced enrolment in MAGNITUDE in ATTR-CM, and began providing investigators with **HLA genotyping results** for patients enrolled or entering screening — which matters because the in vivo product depends on the patient's HLA genotype.
- **Registry (v2 API; robots.txt read first this session):**
  - NCT06634420 (HAELO, Phase 3 in HAE) — active not recruiting, 80 estimated, sponsor Intellia Therapeutics, actual start 2025-01-15, estimated primary completion 2026-02-10, last update posted 2026-10-08, 29 sites (Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Netherlands, New Zealand among them).
  - NCT05120830 (lonvo-z Phase 1/2 in adults with HAE) — active not recruiting, 37 estimated, actual start 2021-12-10, estimated primary completion 2024-04-04, last update posted 2026-09-25, nine sites.
  - NCT06128629 (MAGNITUDE, Phase 3, ATTR-CM) — recruiting, 1,200 estimated, 138 sites, actual start 2023-12-13, estimated primary completion 2027-12, last update posted 2026-11-04.
  - NCT06672237 (MAGNITUDE-2, Phase 3, ATTRv-PN) — active not recruiting, 67 estimated, actual start 2024-11-22, estimated primary completion 2027-07, last update posted 2026-11-05 — the same day as this release.
- **Financials:** cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of **$614.8 million** at September 30, 2026, expected to fund operations at least into 2028 and beyond the anticipated launch, plus a non-dilutive senior secured term loan facility with OrbiMed of up to **$400 million**. Net loss $127.5 million (vs $101.3 million), R&D $83.3 million, G&A $46.1 million, collaboration revenue $7.2 million (vs $13.8 million) on lower Regeneron revenue. A new Chief Medical Officer, Jing L. Marantz, M.D., Ph.D., was appointed in October.
- **Why it belongs here:** the site had **no prior coverage of Intellia, lonvo-z, nex-z, KLKB1 or HAE** — a genuine zero-coverage gap rather than a repeat. This is the nearest the field has come to an approved in vivo genome-editing product: an LNP-delivered edit given once, as an outpatient procedure, with no manufacturing slot per patient and no autologous supply chain.
- **Boundary carried in the item:** the PDUFA date is a target action date, not an approval; the "first in vivo CRISPR therapy" and "only one-time treatment for HAE" phrasings are the company's, and the comparison set is a long list of prophylactic biologics and oral kallikrein inhibitors (donidalorsen, sebetralstat, KVD900, lanadelumab and others all appear in the registry) rather than an empty field.
