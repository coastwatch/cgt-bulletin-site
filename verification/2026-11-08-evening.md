### 1. CRISPR Therapeutics: zugo-cel data across three refractory rheumatologic autoimmune diseases

- **Primary source:** [CRISPR Therapeutics release, November 8, 2026 (GlobeNewswire)](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/08/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/crispr-therapeutics-provides-update-on-zugocabtagene-geleucel-zugo-cel-in-refractory-rheumatologic-autoimmune-diseases/2464772) — HTTP 200, body read directly (GlobeNewswire's own search and RSS both block, so the Manila Times syndication mirror is the readable original text and is labelled as the company release).
- **Systemic sclerosis (poster at ACR Convergence 2026):** as of a July 21, 2026 data cut-off, seven SSc patients dosed at the 100 million CAR+ cell level had at least three months of follow-up. **83% (5 of 6) of patients who received standard lymphodepletion achieved an rCRISS-25 response at Month 3**, including two who reached the more stringent rCRISS-50; **all five responders have maintained their response**, four of them beyond six months. The seventh patient, who received **reduced** lymphodepletion, showed improvement in multiple rCRISS components at Month 3 but did not meet response criteria.
- **Basket-wide update, as of October 15, 2026: 21 patients dosed** — four SLE, eight SSc, nine idiopathic inflammatory myopathy. **All nine IIM patients** (including two with immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy, two with dermatomyositis, five with antisynthetase syndrome) achieved a moderate or major Total Improvement Score response. In SLE, **two of three evaluable patients have maintained drug-free DORIS remission through at least Month 9**, one beyond a year.
- **Safety — the strongest off-the-shelf autoimmune CAR T-cell safety profile to date:** well tolerated in **all 21 patients**, with no dose-limiting toxicities, **no CRS above Grade 2, no ICANS, no IEC-HS, no severe infections, no clinically significant hypogammaglobulinaemia, no prolonged cytopenias, no GvHD and no deaths** reported to date. Dose escalation continues, with the six patients at the 200 million CAR+ cell level showing higher expansion than at 100 million.
- **Registry (v2 API; robots.txt read first this session):** rheumatology basket NCT06925542 — recruiting Phase 1, 80 estimated, sponsor CRISPR Therapeutics, actual start 2025-03-10, estimated primary completion 2031-12-31, last update posted 2026-10-26, 17 sites in Germany, Spain and the United States. Hematology basket (ITP, warm AIHA) NCT07804004 — not yet recruiting, 220 estimated, actual start 2026-11-01. Autoimmune neurologic basket NCT07549698 — recruiting, 60 estimated, actual start 2026-07-17, 12 sites. Immuno-oncology NCT05643742 — recruiting, 120 estimated, seven sites in Australia and the United States.
- **Boundaries carried:** the SSc figures come from a July 21 cut-off at one dose level with six patients meeting response criteria — single-arm, open-label, company-reported, no comparator, and the deeper cut is seven patients. **Response appears contingent on lymphodepletion**: the one patient who received reduced conditioning did not respond, a signal in a sample of one. Registry records carry no posted results. The site covered zugo-cel only as a *scheduled* ACR presentation in the Nov. 2 cycle; this is the data.

### 2. Cabaletta Bio: rese-cel holds responses without immunosuppression for a year, with a 71-patient safety dataset

- **Primary source:** [Cabaletta Bio release, November 8, 2026 (GlobeNewswire)](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/08/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/cabaletta-bio-presents-new-rese-cel-phase-12-clinical-data-at-acr-convergence-2026-featuring-immunomodulator-free-durability-and-favorable-benefit-risk-profile-across-autoimmune-diseases/2464767) — HTTP 200, body read directly.
- **Dermatomyositis:** **100% (6 of 6) of patients maintained immunomodulator-free, moderate-to-major Total Improvement Score responses through one year**, with 83% (5/6) reaching that level as early as **Week 16** — the timepoint corresponding to the primary endpoint of the ongoing registrational dermatomyositis and antisynthetase syndrome cohort — and most also steroid-free at Week 52. Improvements in MMT-8 muscle strength and CDASI-A skin involvement.
- **Lupus nephritis (poster, abstract 1222):** **100% (5/5) of patients who had an optional repeat kidney biopsy achieved DORIS remission off all immunomodulators on no or low-dose steroids, and 100% (5/5) showed histologic response or remission.** Cabaletta argues these data matter for patients who show histologic response without meeting complete renal response criteria, where persistent proteinuria reflects structural kidney damage predating treatment.
- **Systemic sclerosis:** **71% (5/7) achieved immunomodulator-free rCRISS-50 and 86% (6/7) rCRISS-25 at one year**, with **9% improvement in mean percent-predicted forced vital capacity among the five patients with interstitial lung disease**, plus improvement on the modified Rodnan Skin Score. A registrational SSc cohort focused on ILD is enrolling.
- **Safety across 71 patients spanning myositis, systemic sclerosis, lupus and myasthenia gravis:** **94% (67 of 71) no or Grade 1 CRS; Grade 2 CRS in 6% (4 of 71); 96% (68 of 71) no ICANS; no IEC-HS.** Cabaletta attributes the profile to construct and production design — fully human CD19 binder, weight-based dosing, standard nine-day manufacturing process.
- **Registry (v2 API; robots.txt read first this session):** SLE NCT06121297 — recruiting Phase 1/2, 28 estimated, actual start 2024-02-16, estimated primary completion 2029-12, last update posted 2026-10-06, 23 sites in Canada, Spain and the United States; IIM NCT06154252 — recruiting Phase 2/3, 74 estimated, 38 sites in Germany, Italy, the United Kingdom and the United States; SSc NCT06328777 — recruiting Phase 2/3, 37 estimated, 10 US sites; MG NCT06359041 — recruiting Phase 1/2, 24 estimated, 17 US sites; **RESET-MS NCT07006805 — withdrawn**.
- **Boundaries carried:** open-label, single-arm Phase 1/2 cohorts with a September 4, 2026 data cut-off and no comparator arm; the biopsy results are from five patients who chose an optional repeat biopsy; conference data, not yet a peer-reviewed paper. The site covered rese-cel before only as a commercial supply deal; this is its clinical data.

## Notes from this cycle

- **Two autoimmune CAR T-cell readouts landed on the same day, and the contrast is the story.** zugo-cel is allogeneic and off-the-shelf with the cleanest safety denominator in autoimmune disease (21 patients, no CRS above Grade 2, no ICANS, no IEC-HS); rese-cel is autologous with the largest safety denominator (71 patients, no ICANS, no IEC-HS) and a year of durability off all immunosuppression. Publishing them together lets a reader see the trade rather than two unrelated company stories.
- **A conditional response is worth reporting even in a sample of one.** zugo-cel's SSc responses were seen in patients who received standard lymphodepletion, and the one patient who received reduced conditioning did not respond — a design dependency that a headline response rate hides. The item states it.
- **The company release's own cut-off dates differ from its announcement date.** zugo-cel's SSc data are cut at July 21, 2026 while the release is dated November 8, and rese-cel's cut-off is September 4, 2026. Both are carried in the items so a reader can judge the lag between data and publication.
