## Published

### 1. TriPRIL — a BCMA/TACI dual-targeted CAR T-cell, first-in-human Phase 1 in *The Lancet*

- **Primary source:** Puliafito et al., "Natural ligand-based CAR T-cell therapy targeting BCMA and TACI for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (TriPRIL): a non-randomised, phase 1, first-in-human trial," *The Lancet*, published Nov. 17, 2026 — doi 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)01806-4, PMID **43044617**. The full PubMed record was retrieved through eutils and the abstract read directly.
- **Efficacy figures — attributed to the sponsor institution's release, not the abstract.** The abstract reports safety, dose selection and trial design but **does not state response rates**. The 12-of-15 (80%) response and 8-of-15 (53%) no-signs-of-active-myeloma figures come from the Mass General Brigham Cancer Institute release dated Nov. 17, 2026, read via [RegMedNet's republication](https://www.regmednet.com/tripril-dual-targeted-car-t-therapy-shows-promise-in-treatment-resistant-multiple-myeloma/) (HTTP 200, which states "Original story from Mass General Brigham Cancer Institute (MA, USA)"). The item states this attribution explicitly rather than presenting the numbers as the abstract's.
- **Design, from the abstract:** first-in-human, open-label, dose-escalation and dose-expansion Phase 1 at Massachusetts General Hospital. **The primary endpoint is the incidence of study-related adverse events and dose-limiting toxicities — not response rate.** 16 patients enrolled and leukapheresed Sept. 17, 2021 – Jan. 27, 2026; 15 infused; 6 at 1.0 × 10⁸ and 9 at 3.0 × 10⁸ cells; 10 of 15 (67%) female; median age 69 (IQR 65–75); 9 of 15 (60%) previously received and progressed after BCMA-directed CAR T; 4 of 15 (27%) previously received a BCMA- or GPRC5D-directed bispecific T-cell engager; data cutoff March 24, 2026.
- **Safety, from the abstract:** no dose-limiting toxicities at either dose; no treatment-related SAEs; no infusion-related reactions; no biphasic cytokine elevation; no ICANS and no IEC-HS; CRS in 13 of 15 (87%) but grade 1 or 2 only; one grade 2 ICANS and one grade 2 IEC-HS, both at 3.0 × 10⁸, both resolved with corticosteroids; neutropenia 14/15 (93%), nausea 12/15 (80%), hypogammaglobulinaemia 8/15 (53%), fatigue 7/15 (47%), fever 7/15 (47%); non-haematological grade ≥3 events in 5 (33%), all resolved; no treatment-related deaths. **Recommended Phase 2 dose: 3.0 × 10⁸ cells.**
- **Registry (ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API; robots.txt read before the record reads this session):** **NCT05020444** — active not recruiting, Phase 1, **16 participants actual**, lead sponsor Marcela V. Maus, M.D., Ph.D. (class Other), actual start Oct. 5, 2021, estimated primary completion and completion Feb. 24, 2028, **one location** (Massachusetts General Hospital Cancer Center, Boston, MA, US), last update posted Aug. 7, 2026. The registry carries no corporate sponsor — this was an academic investigator study.
- **The company:** Caronilex ([caronilex.com](https://caronilex.com/), HTTP 200), whose site describes it as "Advancing Scalable Dual Targeting CAR-Based Therapies." STAT reported the programme on Nov. 23 ("New biotech aims to develop next generation CAR-T for multiple myeloma"); the STAT piece is subscriber-only, and the item does not rely on it for any figure.
- **Limits stated in the item:** 15 patients, single-arm, non-randomised, data cutoff March 24, 2026, roughly three months of follow-up, so response duration is unknown; and the CR/ORR figures are the sponsor institution's, not the abstract's.

### 2. CARsgen's CT1182 — first clinical data for an in vivo CD19/CD20 CAR T-cell

- **Primary sources:** [CARsgen release via PR Newswire, Nov. 22, 2026, 05:30 ET](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/carsgen-presents-first-clinical-data-on-its-in-vivo-car-t-product-candidate-ct1182-302947651.html) (HTTP 200, body read directly) and the [same release on CARsgen's own newsroom](https://www.carsgen.com/en/news/2026-11-22/) (HTTP 200, body read directly). Both were read; the two texts agree.
- **Data:** six patients — five DLBCL, one blastoid mantle cell lymphoma; all aggressive subtypes, Ann Arbor Stage IV, median 3 prior lines, median total tumour burden 1,927.5 mm², two with prior T-cell engager therapy. Dose-exploration range 1.2 × 10⁸ to 7.2 × 10⁸ TU as of Oct. 31, 2026. Among six efficacy-evaluable patients: **three CR and two PR, all ongoing at cutoff**; CR at the starting dose, longest response nearly five months; one CR in a TCE-refractory patient; no effective CAR T-cell expansion in another TCE-refractory patient (T-cell exhaustion and CD20 antigen loss). Safety in six DLT-evaluable patients: **no treatment-related SAEs, no DLTs, no IRRs**; no biphasic cytokine elevation, no IEC-HS, no ICANS; only Grade 1 CRS; one Grade 3 leukopenia that could not be fully ruled out as disease-related. Median follow-up ~3.2 months.
- **Manufacturing angle carried:** a single intravenous infusion generates CAR T cells in situ **without lymphodepletion**, removing apheresis, expansion and release testing from the product's supply chain; complete B-cell aplasia in all responders; and the company reads the absence of IRRs and biphasic cytokine elevation as supporting **outpatient administration**.
- **Registry (ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API):** **NCT07548697** — recruiting, **early Phase 1**, **24 participants estimated**, single-arm open-label dose-exploring study of CT1182 in r/r B-NHL. **Lead sponsor Union Hospital, Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology** (class Other) with **CARsgen Life Sciences Co., Ltd. as collaborator**; one location in Wuhan, China; start Apr. 30, 2026; estimated primary completion and completion Dec. 31, 2028; last update posted Apr. 23, 2026. The company's own release calls this an investigator-initiated trial, and the registry structure confirms it.
- **Not verified, and therefore not claimed:** the release names **no presentation venue, no abstract number and no peer-reviewed publication**. ASH 2026 has not yet taken place at the time of this cycle, so the item does not attribute the data to any meeting.
- **Company-stated outlook, labelled as such:** CARsgen expects to file for a registrational clinical trial in the first half of 2027.
- **Duplication check:** grepped all five pages for CT1182, KJ-C2529, CARvivo, CARsgen, in vivo CAR-T. The site carries two prior CARsgen items — satri-cel's inclusion in China's commercial insurance drug list and satri-cel's adjuvant gastric data — neither of which covers CT1182 or the CARvivo platform.
