- **Reporting read in full:** [BioPharma Dive, October 27, 2026](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/artiva-discontinue-nk-cell-therapy-autoimmune-patient-death/872873/), HTTP 200. Reports two deaths in the Phase 2a autoimmune study. An investigator linked one leukoencephalopathy event to fludarabine used in lymphodepletion. For a second sudden death about two months after treatment, the cause is undetermined; an investigator reportedly ruled out AlloNK and other regimen components. FDA requested a pause after the first event. Artiva stopped planned enrollment/dosing in a planned Phase 3 study, citing inadequate response durability; it will discontinue AlloNK, reduce staff by about 94%, and review strategic alternatives.
- **Registry:** [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06991114](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06991114), API/page HTTP 200. Phase 2, estimated enrollment 90, status RECRUITING, last update posted July 31, 2026. The registry has not caught up with the October 27 announcement; this mismatch is explicit in the item.
- **Scope and interpretation:** donor-derived, non-genetically modified cord-blood NK-cell therapy; in scope as a cell-therapy development. No inference that this is a verdict on autologous CAR-T or its manufacturing demand. Causality assessments are attributed to investigators via company statements/reporting, not presented as independently established.
