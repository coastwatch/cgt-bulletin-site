### 1. Anocca escalates ANOC-001 into the high-dose cohort and advances a multi-product VIDAR-1 franchise

- **Primary source:** [Anocca AB release via PR Newswire, November 23, 2026, 04:00 ET](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/anocca-announces-dose-escalation-and-advances-multi-product-vidar-1-clinical-programme-in-pancreatic-cancer-302947031.html) — HTTP 200, body read directly.
- **The milestone:** "ANOC-001 was administered to the first patient in the Phase 1 high-dose cohort following approval from the independent safety monitoring committee (SMC) to escalate the dose after its review of low-dose cohort data."
- **The other two products, which are the substantive part of the story:**
  - **ANOC-001B** — "introduces a set of new manufacturing technologies and process improvements that reduce manufacturing cycle time and cost," and the company has "submitted a regulatory amendment to European regulators for inclusion into the VIDAR-1 clinical programme." The release also quotes Anocca's CTO on having "improved our non-viral gene editing manufacturing technology to reduce both cost and production time." Both the manufacturing claim and the amendment are company statements; the item labels them as such and does not call the amendment an approval.
  - **ANOC-002** — a "first-in-class TCR-T cell therapy product targeting KRAS G12V A*03"; preclinical data were presented at the **Society for Immunotherapy of Cancer (SITC) Annual Meeting 2026**, and it is "planned for inclusion into VIDAR-1 in 2027." **No abstract number or poster citation appears in the release, so the item states none.**
- **Registry (ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API; robots.txt read before the record reads this session):** **NCT07145450** — recruiting, Phase 1/2, "Master Protocol for Open-Label, Multi-Centre, Single-Arm Sub-Studies, First in Human Clinical Studies of TCR-T Therapy (Autologous TCR-Modified T-Cell Therapy) Targeting Mutated Kirsten Rat Sarcoma (mutKRAS)," **96 participants estimated**, lead sponsor **Anocca AB** (class Other Government), actual start July 3, 2025, estimated primary completion April 2, 2030 and estimated completion July 31, 2030, **10 sites across Denmark, Germany, the Netherlands and Sweden**, last update posted June 8, 2026, condition PDAC. The release describes Phase I as running at eight university hospitals across four countries, consistent with the record.
- **What the release does not say, and the item therefore does not claim:** no efficacy data, response rates or patient outcomes for the high-dose cohort — the escalation rationale given is safety and tolerability from the low-dose cohort, not evidence of benefit.
- **Why this is in scope and why it is a manufacturing story:** TCR-T cell therapy for a mutated oncoprotein requires a personalised, HLA-matched and mutation-matched autologous product; Anocca manufactures its candidates with **non-viral gene editing at its own in-house cGMP facility in Södertälje, Sweden**, and the release foregrounds cycle time and cost as the thing being engineered down. It is a product-line/umbrella-trial commercial model rather than a single-asset programme, and this bulletin has never covered Anocca.

### 2. Natera and KSQ Therapeutics collaborate on ctDNA monitoring for the KSQ-004EX trial

- **Primary source:** [Natera / KSQ Therapeutics release (Business Wire, carried by BioSpace), November 23, 2026](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/natera-and-ksq-therapeutics-collaborate-on-phase-i-ii-ksq-004ex-study-leveraging-nateras-signatera-test-for-treatment-response-monitoring) — HTTP 200, body read directly. Natera's own press-releases index (https://www.natera.com/press-releases) was also fetched; the item's link points at the Business Wire copy because it is the version whose full text could be read.
- **What the collaboration is:** Natera will "conduct retrospective analyses of patient samples collected from the clinical trial at multiple timepoints, including at baseline, to understand how treatment with KSQ-004EX impacts circulating tumor DNA levels and to identify potential correlates with clinical responses," using **Signatera™ Genome with Phased and Structural variants**. The item states plainly that this is **retrospective analysis on banked samples, not a prospective companion endpoint**, and that it does not change the trial's primary endpoints.
- **The therapy:** KSQ-004EX is "an investigational engineered autologous tumor infiltrating lymphocyte (eTIL) therapy in which the genes encoding SOCS1 and Regnase-1 are inactivated using CRISPR/Cas9."
- **Registry (ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API; robots.txt read before the record reads this session):** **NCT06598371** — recruiting, Phase 1/2, "A Phase 1/2 Study of KSQ-004EX, Autologous Tumor Infiltrating Lymphocytes Engineered to Inactivate Genes Encoding SOCS1 and Regnase-1, in Patients With Select Advanced Solid Tumors," **141 participants estimated**, **lead sponsor M.D. Anderson Cancer Center** (class Other) with **KSQ Therapeutics, Inc. as collaborator** — an investigator-led academic trial with the company in the collaborator role — actual start November 21, 2024, estimated primary completion August 1, 2039, **one site** in the United States, last update posted November 2, 2026. Tumour types per the release: late-stage metastatic melanoma, non-small cell lung cancer, head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, colorectal cancer, pancreatic cancer and cervical cancer.
- **What is not verified:** the release names no results, no ctDNA findings and no biomarker–response correlation. The item says so explicitly.
- **Duplication check:** grepped all five pages for "KSQ", "KSQ-004EX", "Natera", "Signatera", "SOCS1" and "Regnase" — zero hits.
